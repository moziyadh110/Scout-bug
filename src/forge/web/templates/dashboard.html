Nightly: propose → probe → compose

Forge rewrites its own playbook, then proves it got better.

A reasoner model reads failed runs and proposes edits to prompts, retries and memory. Each edit is scored on 8 train tasks, the winners are combined and scored on all 12, and the result is kept only if neither split gets worse. The 4 holdout tasks are never shown to the reasoner.
